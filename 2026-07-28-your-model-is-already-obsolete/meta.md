  In the last month alone: Opus 5, Sonnet 5, Grok 4.5, Kimi K3, GPT-5.6, Gemini 3.6. Any one of them might make your agent smarter or your system cheaper. That's the fun part. The less fun part is that every model you're running in production today just got a month closer to its retirement date.
